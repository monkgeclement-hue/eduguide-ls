Your data and your choices

Updated 14 September 2026.

What you provide

Your account can contain contact details, grades, interests, optional household or support information, documents, saved programmes, application progress, and chat messages. Enter only what you need for guidance. You can enter grades manually instead of uploading a document.

Who can access it

Your profile is available to you and authorized system administrators. An assigned counsellor can see guidance information only after you grant access, and you can pause that access. Institution accounts review their own catalogue proposals; they do not receive access to all student profiles.

AI and document processing

When you request AI guidance, relevant profile, programme, document summaries, and recent conversation context are sent to the configured AI provider: Google Gemini by default, or OpenAI when configured by the operator. Images and scanned documents may be sent to Google Gemini for text extraction. Do not upload unrelated sensitive records. Review suggested grades before accepting them.

Storage and shared devices

Account data is saved on the server. The app keeps local profile copies for recovery during connection problems. Signing out clears EduGuide's local browser caches; session credentials are held for the tab session. Always sign out on a shared device. Closing a tab alone does not clear saved profile copies.

Export and deletion

In your Profile, use Export my data to download your profile, chat, and document metadata. Download original documents separately. Student accounts can use Delete my account and confirm their password. This removes the active account, documents, chat, counsellor records, and linked recommendation history. Staff accounts require the system owner to manage removal.

Older recommendation records that predate account-linked history cannot always be attributed to an account automatically. They expire under the 30-day recommendation-history cleanup. Operational backups are separate from active storage and are controlled by the hosting operator; deletion does not claim to erase an existing backup immediately.

Guidance limitations

Missing admissions evidence is labelled as unknown. Career and skill suggestions are not verified curriculum outcomes. Funding preparation is not a prediction of sponsorship approval. Confirm current admission and sponsorship rules with the relevant institution or sponsor.
